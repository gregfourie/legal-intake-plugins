## Jurisdiction notes — South Africa
- Courts and tribunals: Constitutional Court, Supreme Court of Appeal, High Court (divisions), Labour Appeal Court, Labour Court, Magistrates' Courts, CCMA, bargaining councils, AFSA and private arbitration.
- Professional split: advocates (counsel) briefed by instructing attorneys.
- Citation style: law-report form, e.g. `2024 (1) SA 100 (SCA)`; neutral form, e.g. `[2007] ZACC 22`.
- Spelling: British / South African.
- Case-law source: SAFLII (see caselaw-fetch/references/sources.md).
- Email boilerplate common in SA: POPIA notices (Protection of Personal Information Act), FAIS disclaimers (Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act).
- Email header labels: Afrikaans — `Van:` From, `Aan:` To, `Onderwerp:` Subject, `Gestuur:` / `Datum:` Sent / Date.
